What's inside · by access level

Every level unlocks everything below it — and more.

Access is enforced by hierarchy tier. Producer titles (Regional Producer, Partner, Senior Partner…) are display names carried on top of these four levels.

LEVEL 1

Agent

The nightly engine
  • Executive Dashboard — live ALP, Sits & Sales with daily / weekly / monthly views and a 30-day read-only history
  • Nightly Pulse — all 14 numbers in a guided stepper, Sets through Gross ALP
  • 9 PM gate warnings + the Midnight Miracle window (12–6 AM)
  • Submission streaks & Player's Club ($10K day) recognition
  • Platinum Wall — top Vets, top Rookies, Platinum Rule honorees with tap-to-call contact cards
  • Live agency sales ticker
  • Production History — personal 13-week ALP, sales & close-rate charts
  • Premier Shoutouts feed + nominate anyone for the Platinum Rule
  • One-tap upline contact card
LEVEL 2

GA / SA

CoExecutive Producer · Regional Producer
EVERYTHING IN AGENT, PLUS
  • TEAM tab — full downline roster with Gross/Net ALP, sales, close rate, avg deal & coaching alert flags
  • Sort, search, and per-agent contact cards with their 13-week production history
  • Past-week picker — rewind the board to any archived reporting week
  • MISSING TONIGHT queue — see who hasn't pulsed and ENTER ALL on their behalf (up to 7 days back)
  • Platinum Nominations inbox — review & endorse (3 endorsements flag for the wall)
LEVEL 3

MGA

Executive Producer
EVERYTHING IN GA / SA, PLUS
  • POST TO PLATINUM WALL — publish threshold-met nominations agency-wide
  • Full-hierarchy rollups — every leader and agent under you, live
  • Downline coaching view — any agent's 13-week trend across your whole hierarchy
LEVEL 4

RGA

Chief Executive Producer
EVERYTHING IN MGA, PLUS
  • Global scope — the entire agency on every screen
  • Manager Command Panel — Net ALP Eraser with mandatory written reason; Gross ALP stays locked on the wall
  • Audit Log — immutable ledger of every adjustment: who, whom, how much, why, when
  • Company Health — per-office trend dashboard: 8–26-week ranges, KPI grid, ALP / sales / close-rate charts
  • Archived week snapshots — side-by-side comparison & WAR-format export
  • Wednesday 2:00 PM weekly lock — the reset that archives each production week
One rule, everywhere: Close Rate = Sales ÷ Sits. N1 visits (medically unqualified) are tallied separately and already excluded from Sits — they never count against an agent. · Reporting cycle runs 6:00 AM–5:59 AM Detroit time. · Admin tools (roster management, WAR report import, office merge) are a separate permission independent of level.
