Troubleshooting · every known snag

If something looks wrong, it's one of these five.

Tap "Show me" to load the exact screen on the phone above, with its fix.

ERROR 1

"Invitation revoked or invalid"

You tapped a TestFlight link from an older email — old invite links die when a new build goes out. Also appears if the link itself was wrong.
FIXGo back to your inbox and open the newest "The new AO Premier agent app is here" email from betatest@hypervividstudios.com. Tap JOIN VIA TESTFLIGHT there — it always carries the current build.
ERROR 2

TestFlight says "Ready To Test"

You opened the TestFlight app directly. On its own it shows nothing — the invite has to arrive through the email link. Don't type anything into Redeem.
FIXSwitch to your inbox, open the newest "The new AO Premier agent app is here" email, tap JOIN VIA TESTFLIGHT — it deep-links into TestFlight with VantageLife attached.
ERROR 3

No invite email at all

If no link ever works and you can't find any invitation, you're probably not on the tester list yet — we have no email on file for you.
FIXSend your onboarding email (form below): name, email, phone, and upline to betatest@hypervividstudios.com. Then watch that inbox for the fresh invite.
ERROR 4

Code page: "Invalid state parameter"

During Google sign-in the browser lands on a raw code page reading "invalid_request … Invalid state parameter." This was a launch-week platform bug — it has been fixed for all affected agents.
FIXClose the browser sheet (✕), return to VantageLife, and tap Sign in with Google again — a fresh attempt goes through. Still stuck? Email betatest@hypervividstudios.com.
ERROR 5

"Account Pending" after sign-in

Two causes: you signed in with Apple (or the wrong Google account), so the email doesn't match the roster — or you don't have an active profile in the app yet.
FIXTap Sign Out, then Sign in with Google using the email your upline has on file. No profile yet? Send the onboarding email below — once you're on the roster, signing in again goes straight to your dashboard.

Need to be added? One email gets you onboarded.

Write to betatest@hypervividstudios.com with exactly four things: your first and last name, the email address to invite, your phone number, and your upline. You'll get a fresh TestFlight invitation at that address.
