How to promote and move people

Both happen on the Team tab, from the person's card. You do it yourself, for your own downline, and the app checks every rule before it goes through. Nothing here needs an admin unless the page says so.

Promote or change a tier

Change TierAlso used to lower a tier. The title changes with it.

  1. Open the Team tab and tap the person's row to open their card.
  2. Tap Change Tier.
  3. Pick the new tier and title in one step: Trainee, Agent, SA, GA or MGA. Their access and their displayed title move together, so nobody ends up with GA access and an Agent title.
  4. Confirm. It applies right away, without the person signing out and back in.
Move to a new upline or office

MoveThe person's office follows their new upline.

  1. Open the Team tab, tap the person's row, then tap Move to a New Upline.
  2. Decide whether their downline goes with them. The switch is on by default. Switched off, the people who report to them stay behind under the moved person's current upline.
  3. Pick the new upline. A pick in another office is marked Moves them to … and is only open to the owner and MJ.
  4. Read the confirmation, which says exactly who moves where, then tap Move.

  • Your own downline only. You can only promote or move people who report up to you. A row marked Team is a teammate you can see, not someone you manage.
  • Strictly below your own tier. SA and GA are the same tier, so an SA or GA can make an Agent, never an SA or GA. An MGA can make an SA or GA, never another MGA. Nobody can mint a peer who would then read their book.
  • Nobody goes above their own upline. To make a GA who reports to an MGA into an MGA, move the GA under an RGA first, then change the tier.
  • Nobody with active reports is lowered. Move their people first; otherwise that branch would go dark to them.
  • Office follows the upline. A person filed under a leader in another office is a data error, so a cross-office move re-homes the person, and with the switch on their whole downline, into the new office. From then on they count toward that office everywhere.
  • Everything is logged. Every tier change and move is written to the audit log with who did it, when, and what changed.
Also for MGAs and RGAs: your own nightly pulse is filed as Not In Field automatically at 6:30 AM if you have not entered numbers, so there is no 9 PM banner, no streak and no missing-pulse flag on your row. Enter your own numbers any time and they replace the automatic entry. Agents, SAs and GAs still enter every night.
